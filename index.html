About Us

Nurturing Hope, Building Futures

Progressive Ideal Trust Orphanage was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to provide every child with the love, care, and opportunities they deserve to thrive.

For over 25 years, we have been a beacon of hope for children who have lost their families or cannot be cared for by their parents. We provide not just shelter, but a loving home where children can grow, learn, and dream.

Quality Education

Providing access to quality education and skill development programs for every child.

Healthcare Support

Regular health check-ups, medical care, and nutritional support for all children.

Safe Shelter

A nurturing home environment with dedicated caregivers and modern facilities.
