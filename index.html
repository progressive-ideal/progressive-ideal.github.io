About Us

Nurturing Hope, Building Futures

Progressive Ideal Trust Orphanage was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to provide every child with the love, care, and opportunities they deserve to thrive.

For over 25 years, we have been a beacon of hope for children who have lost their families or cannot be cared for by their parents. We provide not just shelter, but a loving home where children can grow, learn, and dream.

Quality Education

Providing access to quality education and skill development programs for every child.

Healthcare Support

Regular health check-ups, medical care, and nutritional support for all children.

Safe Shelter

A nurturing home environment with dedicated caregivers and modern facilities.

How We Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our organization and how you can help.

You can donate online through our secure donation platform, via bank transfer, or in person at our facility. We accept one-time donations as well as monthly recurring contributions. All donations are tax-deductible.

Yes! We welcome volunteers with various skills. You can help with teaching, mentoring, organizing activities, administrative work, or maintenance. Please contact us to learn about current opportunities and the application process.

Approximately 85% of donations go directly to programs supporting the children - education, healthcare, nutrition, and daily care. The remaining 15% covers essential operational costs. We publish annual reports detailing how funds are allocated.

Yes, we offer a child sponsorship program where you can support a specific child's education and care. You'll receive regular updates on their progress and can even exchange letters. Contact us to learn more about sponsorship opportunities.

Yes, Progressive Ideal Trust Orphanage is a registered charitable organization. All donations are tax-deductible to the full extent allowed by law. You will receive a receipt for your donation that can be used for tax purposes.
