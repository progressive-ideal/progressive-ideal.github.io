Volunteer Appreciation Day

On April 5th, we held our annual Volunteer Appreciation Day to honor the incredible individuals who dedicate their time, skills, and hearts to the children at Progressive Ideal Yatimkhana. Over 50 volunteers were recognized for their outstanding contributions throughout the year.

The event was filled with heartfelt speeches, touching stories, and joyous celebrations. Several volunteers shared their experiences, describing how working with our children has transformed their own lives. "I came to give, but I received so much more in return," said long-time volunteer Grace Mwangi, who has been with us for eight years.

This year, we introduced the "Champion of Hope" awards, given to volunteers who have gone above and beyond in their service. The recipients were selected by the children themselves, making the recognition even more meaningful. Each awardee received a handcrafted certificate made by the children.

Our volunteers contribute in countless ways — from tutoring and mentoring to organizing activities, providing medical care, and helping with daily operations. Their selfless dedication allows us to provide the level of care and attention that every child deserves. If you're interested in volunteering, we'd love to hear from you!
