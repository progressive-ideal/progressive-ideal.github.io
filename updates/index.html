Latest Updates

Stay informed about our latest news, events, milestones, and the impact your support is making in the lives of children.

April 2026

Construction April 18, 2026

New Classroom Building Progress

Our new classroom building is taking shape! Thanks to generous donations, the foundation and walls are complete, and we're on track to finish by summer. This will allow us to accommodate 50 more students.
Community April 5, 2026

Volunteer Appreciation Day

We celebrated our incredible volunteers who dedicate their time and hearts to our children. Over 50 volunteers were honored for their contributions, sharing stories of hope and transformation.

March 2026

Events March 22, 2026

Annual Sports Day Celebration

Our children showcased their athletic talents at the annual sports day. From relay races to football matches, the day was filled with teamwork, laughter, and unforgettable moments of joy.
Healthcare March 10, 2026

Health Camp for Children

A comprehensive health camp was organized with support from City Hospital. All 150 children received complete health check-ups, dental exams, and vaccinations to ensure their well-being.

February 2026

Education February 20, 2026

Partnership with Local Schools

We're thrilled to announce a new partnership with three local schools, enabling our children to attend mainstream education with full tuition support and after-school tutoring programs.
Outreach February 5, 2026

Winter Clothing Drive Success

Our winter clothing drive collected over 500 jackets, 300 pairs of shoes, and countless warm accessories. Every child at our facility now has proper winter gear, and we shared surplus with nearby communities.
