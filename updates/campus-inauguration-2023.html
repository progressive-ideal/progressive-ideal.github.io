Milestones 8 April 2023

Our Building Was Inaugurated in April 2023

The orphanage building was inaugurated on 8 April 2023, following its construction in 2021. Progressive Ideal Yatimkhana itself was established in 2013.

The January–June 2026 report describes a two-storey building with residential, office, teaching, kitchen and dining facilities on the orphanage’s own site in Kurapara, Pangsha, Rajbari.

Source: January–June 2026 orphanage report.
