Health Camp for Children

On March 10th, we organized a comprehensive health camp in partnership with City Hospital, bringing a team of 15 medical professionals to our campus. All 150 children at our facility received thorough health check-ups, ensuring their continued well-being and early detection of any health concerns.

The health camp covered a wide range of services including general physical examinations, dental check-ups, eye exams, hearing tests, and vaccination updates. Each child received a personalized health report card, and follow-up appointments have been scheduled for those needing additional care.

Dr. Aisha Patel, who led the medical team, noted that the overall health of our children is excellent — a testament to our nutrition program and daily healthcare routines. A few children were identified as needing corrective lenses, and we've already arranged for them to receive glasses within the next two weeks.

We're deeply grateful to City Hospital and all the medical professionals who volunteered their time for this camp. Regular health monitoring is essential for growing children, and events like these ensure that no health issue goes undetected. Our next health camp is planned for September 2026.
