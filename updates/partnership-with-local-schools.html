Education February 20, 2026

Partnership with Local Schools

We are thrilled to announce an exciting new partnership with three esteemed local schools — Greenfield Academy, Sunrise Preparatory, and Heritage High School. This collaboration will enable our children to attend mainstream schools with full tuition support, marking a significant step forward in our education program.

Under this partnership, 45 of our children will enroll in these schools starting from the next academic year. The partnership covers full tuition, textbooks, uniforms, and school supplies. Additionally, each school has committed to providing after-school tutoring sessions and extracurricular activities for our children.

This initiative represents our belief that integrating orphaned children into mainstream education helps build social skills, confidence, and a sense of belonging that is crucial for their development. The schools have been incredibly welcoming, organizing orientation sessions and buddy programs to ensure our children feel at home from day one.

We extend our heartfelt thanks to the administrators, teachers, and parent communities of Greenfield Academy, Sunrise Preparatory, and Heritage High School for opening their doors and hearts to our children. Together, we are building bridges that will last a lifetime.
