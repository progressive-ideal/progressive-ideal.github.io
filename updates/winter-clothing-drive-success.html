Outreach February 5, 2026

Winter Clothing Drive Success

Our Winter Clothing Drive, which ran throughout January, has been an overwhelming success! Thanks to the incredible generosity of our community, we collected over 500 jackets, 300 pairs of shoes, 200 scarves and gloves, and countless other warm accessories — far exceeding our initial goals.

Every child at Progressive Ideal Yatimkhana now has proper winter gear, including warm jackets, boots, sweaters, and thermal undergarments. The joy on their faces as they tried on their new winter clothes was truly heartwarming. For many of our children, this was the first time they owned a brand-new winter coat.

What makes this drive even more special is that we were able to share the surplus with three neighboring community organizations that support vulnerable families. An additional 200 items were distributed to children in the wider community, extending the warmth and care far beyond our own walls.

We want to express our deepest gratitude to every individual, school, business, and organization that contributed. Special thanks to Riverside Mall for hosting our collection bins, and to QuickDeliver Logistics for providing free transportation of all donated items. Your kindness has made this winter warmer for hundreds of children.
