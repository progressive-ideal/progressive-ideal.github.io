Construction April 18, 2026

New Classroom Building Progress

Our new classroom building is taking shape! Thanks to generous donations from our supporters around the world, the foundation and walls are now complete, and we're on track to finish the entire structure by summer 2026.

This new building will add six modern classrooms, a computer lab, and a library to our campus. Once completed, it will allow us to accommodate 50 more students, bringing our total capacity to over 200 children who can receive quality education in a nurturing environment.

The construction team has been working diligently, and we're pleased to report that we're under budget and ahead of schedule. The roofing phase begins next week, followed by interior work including electrical, plumbing, and painting. We expect to welcome students into the new classrooms by the start of the new academic year in September.

We're incredibly grateful to everyone who has contributed to this project — whether through donations, volunteer labor, or moral support. This building represents more than just bricks and mortar; it represents hope, opportunity, and the belief that every child deserves access to quality education.

Project Highlights

  • 6 new classrooms with modern equipment
  • State-of-the-art computer lab with 30 workstations
  • Dedicated library with over 5,000 books
  • Accessible design for children with disabilities
