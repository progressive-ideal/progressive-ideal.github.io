About Us

Our Story of Hope & Care

Established in 2013, Progressive Ideal Yatimkhana provides residential care and educational support in Kurapara, Pangsha, Rajbari, Bangladesh.

Residential Care

The January–June 2026 report records 48 resident students and 48 beds. Support includes accommodation, meals, clothing and healthcare expenses.

Students receive support for their studies alongside daily residential care.

Our Campus

The orphanage stands on its own 66-decimal site. Its two-storey building has an approximately 110×32-foot footprint, eight bedrooms and fourteen toilets.

Facilities include offices, teachers’ space, a multipurpose room, a kitchen and dining space.

Our History

From Humble Beginnings

Progressive Ideal Yatimkhana (প্রগ্রেসিভ আইডিয়াল এতিমখানা), also known as Candemir Orphanage, was established in 2013. It grew from the work of Progressive Ideal Trust, which began in 2007.

Progressive Ideal Academy was established on 1 January 2009. The orphanage building was constructed in 2021 and inaugurated on 8 April 2023.
