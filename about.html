About Us

Our Story of Hope & Care

For over 25 years, Progressive Ideal Trust Orphanage has been a beacon of hope for children in need, providing love, care, and opportunities for a brighter future.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, nurturing environment where every child can thrive. We are committed to offering quality education, healthcare, and emotional support to children who have lost their families or cannot be cared for by their parents.

We believe that every child deserves love, respect, and the opportunity to reach their full potential, regardless of their circumstances.

Our Vision

A world where every child has access to love, education, healthcare, and the opportunity to pursue their dreams. We envision communities where orphaned and vulnerable children are integrated, valued, and empowered.

We strive to be a model institution that inspires others to create similar havens of hope for children in need.

Our History

From Humble Beginnings

Progressive Ideal Trust Orphanage was founded in 1999 by a group of compassionate individuals who saw the urgent need to help children who had been left without families. What started as a small shelter with just 10 children has grown into a comprehensive care facility.

Over the years, we have expanded our programs to include education, healthcare, vocational training, and community integration support. Today, we care for over 150 children and have helped more than 1,000 children transition to independent, successful lives.

1999

Founded with 10 children in a small rented house

2005

Built our permanent facility with capacity for 50 children

2012

Launched education and vocational training programs

2020

Expanded to support 150+ children with modern facilities
